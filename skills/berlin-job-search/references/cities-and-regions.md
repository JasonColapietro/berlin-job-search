# Cities and regions

> Industry, language and cost notes are orientation, not guarantees. Confirm each vacancy's address, German requirements, office days and current rent/commute conditions.

## Berlin — deepest coverage

Clusters include startups, fintech, climate/energy, AI, SaaS, media and international technology teams ([Berlin Partner](https://www.berlin-partner.de/en/)). Tech in Berlin's Q1 2026 snapshot counted 155 software-engineering and 39 junior roles across 380+ startups; senior/management roles were more numerous ([Q1 report](https://www.techinberlin.com/news/berlin-tech-hiring-in-the-first-quarter-of-2026-what-the-numbers-show)). On 2026-10-23, Job Drop Berlin listed 2,361 live positions across 401 startups: 53% marked English-speaking and 395 (17%) stated salary ([Daily Drop](https://jobdropberlin.com/daily)). These are snapshots, not forecasts.

Employers include Delivery Hero, Zalando, N26, Deutsche Bahn, Charité and BVG. Local sources: [BerlinStartupJobs](https://berlinstartupjobs.com/), [Daily Berlin Jobs](https://daily-berlin-jobs.vercel.app/jobs.md) (curated Berlin tech engineering, daily update), [Tech in Berlin](https://techinberlin.com/), [Job Drop Berlin](https://jobdropberlin.com/), [pegel.berlin](https://pegel.berlin/) and the [Berlin Karriereportal](https://www.berlin.de/karriereportal/). English is more common in startups/international teams; public-facing, legal, healthcare and regulated roles often require German. Housing can constrain location and commute.

For Berlin searches, separate fintech/commerce roles (product, payments, risk, compliance, support and operations), climate/energy variants (energy, heat, solar, mobility, battery, sustainability), AI/research (production, applied, academic and policy), and public/regulated roles. Verify license and German requirements, level and ownership, and whether “Berlin” means an office, a site outside the ring or Germany-wide remote.

## Other city markets

- **Munich:** automotive, mobility, insurance, enterprise software, life sciences, research and industrial tech. Employers include BMW, Siemens, Allianz, Infineon, Munich Re and TUM-affiliated research. German is common in manufacturing, public-facing and local-market jobs; international teams may work in English. Sources: [City industry overview](https://www.munich-business.eu/en/business-location/industries.html), [Munich Startup jobs](https://www.munich-startup.de/jobs/) and [StepStone](https://www.stepstone.de/). Housing is among Germany's most expensive; include commute.
- **Hamburg:** logistics, aviation, media, maritime, renewable energy, commerce and consumer brands; employers include Airbus, Lufthansa Technik, Otto Group, Beiersdorf and About You. English appears in international/technical teams; German is common in operations/customer roles. Sources: [Hamburg Business](https://hamburg-business.com/en/why-hamburg/top-facts-hamburg), [Hamburg Startups](https://www.hamburg-startups.net/) and [BA Jobsuche](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/jobsuche/). Housing can be high near central and waterfront areas.
- **Frankfurt / Rhine-Main:** banking, fintech, consulting, data centers, logistics, pharma and professional services; employers include Deutsche Bank, Commerzbank, ECB-related institutions, Lufthansa and consultancies. English is common in international finance; German often matters in client work/administration. Sources: [Frankfurt business overview](https://frankfurt.de/english/international-city/business-in-frankfurt) (HTTP 403 in automated check), [FrankfurtRheinMain industry profile](https://www.find-it-in-frm.de/en/industries-in-frm), [StepStone](https://www.stepstone.de/) and [Jobware](https://www.jobware.de/). Wider-region commuting may cross state lines.
- **Cologne / Düsseldorf:** media, telecom, insurance, retail, consulting, chemicals and industry; examples include RTL, REWE, Deutsche Telekom and Henkel. German is typical in local customer/media/public-service roles; global teams may use English. Sources: [Cologne/Bonn region](https://www.region-koeln-bonn.de/aufgaben/innovation-wirtschaft/wirtschaftsstandort), [Düsseldorf business overview](https://www.duesseldorf.de/wirtschaft-und-standort) and [Netlight](https://jobs.lever.co/netlight). Verify which city/site and hybrid days; nearby towns may widen options.
- **Stuttgart:** automotive, engineering, automation, software and advanced manufacturing; employers include Mercedes-Benz, Porsche, Bosch and suppliers. German is common in factories, suppliers and stakeholder-facing jobs; global teams may use English. Sources: [Stuttgart Region](https://www.region-stuttgart.de/en/economy/), [Jobware](https://www.jobware.de/) and [INTERAMT](https://www.interamt.de/).
- **Dresden / Leipzig:** Dresden has semiconductor, photonics, research and manufacturing; Leipzig has logistics, automotive, media, energy and services. Examples include GlobalFoundries, Infineon, DHL, BMW and research institutions. Sources: [Dresden location profile](https://www.dresden.de/media/pdf/wirtschaft/2024-09-11_Standortbroschuere_DD_2024_en.pdf), [Leipzig economic profile](https://english.leipzig.de/science-and-economy/economic-profile-and-clusters) and [academics.de](https://www.academics.de/). Costs are often lower than Munich/Frankfurt, but salary and availability differ.
- **Karlsruhe / Darmstadt:** software, cybersecurity, energy, engineering and research; examples include SAP, KIT, ESA/ESOC, Merck and research centers. International research/software jobs may be English-friendly; public-facing/admin roles often need German. Sources: [Karlsruhe innovation profile](https://www.karlsruhe.de/wirtschaft-wissenschaft/wirtschaftsstandort/innovationsstandort), [Darmstadt economic overview](https://www.darmstadt.de/standort/wirtschaft), [Stellenwerk](https://www.stellenwerk.de/) and [EURAXESS](https://euraxess.ec.europa.eu/jobs).

## Match role families to locations

| Role family | Cities to compare | Check in the posting |
|---|---|---|
| Software / data / AI | Berlin, Munich, Hamburg, Frankfurt, Cologne, Karlsruhe, Darmstadt | Internal documentation, customer contact and actual team language. |
| Automotive / embedded / industrial software | Munich, Stuttgart, Dresden/Leipzig, Karlsruhe | OEM vs supplier/factory/consultancy; license, shift and site requirements. |
| Finance / risk / compliance | Frankfurt, Berlin, Munich, Cologne | Client-facing work, regulation and German documentation. |
| Logistics / mobility / aviation | Hamburg, Frankfurt, Leipzig, Berlin | Shifts, travel and airport/port access. |
| Research / academia / PhD | Berlin, Munich, Dresden, Leipzig, Karlsruhe, Darmstadt | Funding duration, publications, teaching and lab language. |
| Public service / healthcare | Every region | Recognition, license, citizenship and language conditions. |

## Search radius and relocation checks

Use these as query expansions, not commute guarantees. Search metros separately where noted; verify office address, transit time and office-day expectations.

| Target | Query variants | Confirm before widening |
|---|---|---|
| Berlin | `Berlin`, `Berlin Metropolitan Area`, `Brandenburg`; remote filter when eligible | Berlin office vs metro site vs Germany-wide remote. |
| Munich | `München`, `Munich`, `Garching`, employer location | Campus/production site, office days and commute. |
| Hamburg | `Hamburg`, `Hamburg Metropolitan Region`, `Norderstedt`, `Lüneburg` | Port/airport/logistics shifts may start outside commute hours. |
| Frankfurt / Rhine-Main | `Frankfurt am Main`, `Frankfurt/Rhein-Main`, `Offenbach`, `Wiesbaden`, `Mainz`, `Darmstadt` | Travel across Hesse, Rhineland-Palatinate or the metro. |
| Cologne / Düsseldorf | `Köln`, `Cologne`, `Düsseldorf`, `Bonn`, `Leverkusen`, `Duisburg` | Separate labor markets, office location, hybrid days and reimbursement. |
| Stuttgart | `Stuttgart`, `Böblingen`, `Esslingen`, `Ludwigsburg`, `Sindelfingen` | Factory/campus shift times; whether a car/licence is truly required. |
| Dresden / Leipzig | Search separately; add `Sachsen` for regional employers | Production/logistics shifts and campus distance. |
| Karlsruhe / Darmstadt | Search separately; add `KIT`, `TU Darmstadt`, `ESA` or employer | Rail connections and research-center/industrial-site location. |

This is a search map, not a city ranking. The same employer may use different salary bands, language requirements and office policies by role/location. Compare live role volume, German level, gross salary after housing/commute, family needs, permit eligibility, professional network and relocation timing. Check current local rent listings and the municipality's Mietspiegel; distinguish **Kaltmiete** from **Warmmiete**. Include deposits, required office days, commute at shift hours, Jobticket/relocation benefits, and remote-country/payroll/time-zone rules. Use a current net-pay calculator. Prefer a primary city and one or two alternatives over applying everywhere.
