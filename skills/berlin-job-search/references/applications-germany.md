# Applications in Germany

> Practical application guidance, not legal advice. The user verifies every claim and personally reviews and submits each application.

## Lebenslauf

German CVs are usually concise, tabular and reverse chronological; one or two pages is a useful target for many roles, while experienced or academic applicants may need more. Put the target role in the first third, use consistent `MM/YYYY` dates, and list title, employer, location and outcome-focused bullets. See [Make it in Germany: application documents](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/working-in-germany/job/application).

- Keep original role and degree names when translation would change their meaning; explain unfamiliar titles briefly.
- Include education, relevant training, skills and defensible CEFR levels. A photo, birth date, marital status and nationality are not universally required; some traditional employers expect a photo, while international technology applications often omit it.
- Use truthful action → scope → result bullets. Example: “Built a monthly operations dashboard for a 12-person team, reducing manual reconciliation time by about 20%.” Use a real, explainable measure; otherwise state scope or audience. Keep employment dates/status consistent with records.
- Keep passport/residence numbers, tax IDs, bank details and references' private contacts off the CV.

## Anschreiben and cover letter

A German letter normally has **Betreff**, formal salutation, a short role-specific opening, evidence linked to the ad, availability/earliest start, salary only if requested, and **Mit freundlichen Grüßen**. Address a named contact only when verified. Use formal **Sie** unless the employer clearly uses another style; use the hiring team's tone/spelling in English. **(verify)** company-specific style. Templates: `assets/anschreiben-template-de.md` and `assets/cover-letter-template-en.md`.

Connect one or two requirements to real examples; do not repeat the CV. Include *Gehaltsvorstellung* only when requested or explicitly approved; if requested without a posted range, use a reasoned gross range, not a regrettable minimum. Before finalizing, check the role/reference number, contact name/salutation, start date or notice period, company facts, names, dates and attachments; read the letter aloud in its target language.

## Files and submission

When one PDF is requested, a common order is cover letter, CV, relevant certificates/degree records, selected Arbeitszeugnisse and role-relevant certificates. Follow the employer's separate-file/portal rules. Use readable names such as `Firstname-Lastname-CV-EN.pdf`; inspect the export on phone and desktop, confirm text is selectable, and remove comments, tracked changes, hidden layers, placeholders and unrelated personal data. Translate certificates only when requested or useful; use certified translations only when the authority/employer requires them. Check recognition early for regulated qualifications.

| Route | Prepare | Final check |
|---|---|---|
| Employer portal | Required files, fields and role/reference number. | Check file type/size, preview uploads, and read privacy/consent text. |
| Direct email | Requested attachments and a concise role/reference subject. | Verify recipient spelling/domain; attach no extra identity documents. |
| Agency/recruiter | CV version approved for sharing and role/company context. | Confirm client identity and whether the CV may be forwarded. |
| Unsolicited application | Specific team/problem and a short relevant message. | Confirm that the employer accepts unsolicited applications and states retention terms. |

Only upload documents relevant to that employer. References and certificates may expose other people's information; get permission. Voluntary demographic/disability fields are the user's choice. Verify a recruiter and the reason before sharing salary, permit, references or personal documents. Save the posting URL, deadline, file versions and submission date in the private tracker. Keep receipt confirmations; do not resend through another channel unless asked.

## Tailor without fabrication

| Posting asks for | Truthful response |
|---|---|
| A tool/method you used | Name it in the relevant bullet and state what you did. |
| A related tool you have not used | State adjacent experience and a learning plan; do not list it as a skill. |
| An unmeasured result | Describe scope, users or process without inventing a percentage. |
| A German credential equivalent | Give the original degree/institution; mention recognition only when verified. |
| A language level | Use a defensible CEFR or working-language description. |

A keyword is not automatically professional experience. The user reviews every CV, letter, form answer and attachment before submission; no sending, uploading or applying without approval for that exact action.

## After submission and other routes

Record date, title, employer, URL, language, attachment versions, promised response date and a human-approved follow-up reminder. For interview travel or application expenses, keep receipts if asking BA/Jobcenter about **Vermittlungsbudget**; eligibility/approval rules apply.

- **Initiativbewerbung:** name a specific team/problem; don't mass-mail generic letters.
- **Referral:** ask for an introduction only where a real connection exists; a referral is not a guarantee.
- **Follow-up:** one polite note after the stated decision date or roughly one to two weeks is an etiquette norm, not a legal deadline.
- **AGG:** the General Equal Treatment Act bars discrimination on protected grounds. See the [Federal Anti-Discrimination Agency overview](https://www.antidiskriminierungsstelle.gov.de/EN/about-discrimination/areas-of-life/work-life/work-life-node.html); seek advice promptly because legal deadlines can be short.
- **Vermittlungsbudget:** BA/Jobcenter may reimburse eligible application or interview-travel costs for qualifying jobseekers; it is discretionary and BA advises applying before costs arise. [BA guide](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/vermittlungsbudget).

## Arbeitszeugnis

Employment certificates are governed by [§109 GewO](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gewo/__109.html). These phrases are conventional shorthand, not a legal interpretation; context and the full certificate matter. Source: [IHK Koblenz](https://www.ihk.de/koblenz/unternehmensservice/recht/arbeitsrecht/arbeitszeugnis-1485780).

| Phrase | Common reading |
|---|---|
| „stets zu unserer vollsten Zufriedenheit“ | Sehr gut |
| „zu unserer vollsten Zufriedenheit“ / „stets zu unserer vollen Zufriedenheit“ | Gut |
| „zu unserer vollen Zufriedenheit“ | Befriedigend |
| „zu unserer Zufriedenheit“ | Ausreichend |
| „war bemüht, … zu unserer Zufriedenheit“ | Often read as poor; seek qualified advice before concluding. |
