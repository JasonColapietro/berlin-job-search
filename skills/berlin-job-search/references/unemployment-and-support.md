# Unemployment and support

> Benefits depend on insurance history, residence, household, income and the individual case. Contact the Agentur für Arbeit or Jobcenter; this overview is not a benefit decision.

## Register on time

Register **arbeitsuchend** no later than three months before a known job end, or within three days of learning of a sooner end. Register separately **arbeitslos** by the first day without work. These registrations are not interchangeable. Sources: [BA job-seeker registration](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/arbeitslos-arbeit-finden/arbeitslosengeld/ihre-schritte-wenn-sie-arbeitslos-werden/wie-sie-sich-arbeitsuchend-melden) and [BA unemployment registration](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/arbeitslos-arbeit-finden/arbeitslosengeld/ihre-schritte-wenn-sie-arbeitslos-werden/wie-sie-sich-arbeitslos-melden). If a deadline may have passed, contact the BA promptly. eServices may require identity verification; see [BA eServices](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/eservices).

## Arbeitslosengeld I (ALG I)

ALG I is insurance-based. Typical eligibility includes unemployment, availability for insured work of at least 15 hours/week, active job search, unemployment registration and the qualifying insurance period. The standard period is generally 12 insured months in the 30 months before unemployment; short fixed-term exceptions and other credited periods can apply. The BA's simplified replacement rate is **60%** of calculated net benefit earnings, or **67%** for a claimant or qualifying spouse/partner with a child; it is not a fixed share of the last payslip. Duration depends on age and insured periods. Sources: [BA entitlement, amount and duration](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/arbeitslos-arbeit-finden/arbeitslosengeld/finanzielle-hilfen/arbeitslosengeld-anspruch-hoehe-dauer) and [BA 2026 leaflet](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/datei/merkblatt-fuer-arbeitslose_ba036520.pdf).

Submit through BA eServices or the responsible office; keep employment dates, wage records, requested documents, registration confirmation and job-search evidence. Tell the BA promptly when a new job starts or address/availability changes; an employer payroll report may not replace your notification. Attend required appointments and keep letters/deadlines. Ask whether foreign work/insurance periods count; the authority decides. If a decision seems wrong, follow its appeal instructions promptly; formal deadlines can be short.

## Resignation, termination and Sperrzeit

Resigning, signing an Aufhebungsvertrag or other conduct may cause a **Sperrzeit** of up to 12 weeks; a documented important reason may avoid it. Before resigning/signing, compare dismissal, mutual termination and resignation with a qualified adviser; check notice, leave, release, severance and non-compete terms. If challenging a dismissal, the BA says the usual filing deadline is three weeks. Source: [BA: ALG I, Sperrzeit, dismissal and severance](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/arbeitslos-arbeit-finden/arbeitslosengeld/das-muessen-sie-beachten/kuendigung-abfindung-freistellung). Get individual advice before acting.

## Grundsicherungsgeld / Jobcenter

From **1 July 2026**, the BA says Grundsicherungsgeld replaced Bürgergeld; older documents may still use the old term. Jobcenters assess household (**Bedarfsgemeinschaft**) income/assets, residence eligibility and local housing costs; there is no universal amount. Non-German eligibility is case-specific. Sources: [BA reform overview](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/grundsicherung-loest-buergergeld-ab) and [needs and components](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/grundsicherung/finanziell-absichern/bedarfe). Contact the Jobcenter promptly if ALG I does not apply or is ending; provide household/housing information only through official channels.

## Training and job-search support

- **Bildungsgutschein:** may fund approved training needed to end/prevent unemployment; ask the BA/Jobcenter before enrolling. [BA guide](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/karriere-und-weiterbildung/bildungsgutschein).
- **Gründungszuschuss:** eligible ALG I recipients starting full-time self-employment may receive six months at the ALG I amount plus €300/month, then possibly nine months at €300/month. It is discretionary, requires a viable plan and usually at least 150 days of entitlement at start; discuss before starting. [BA guide](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/arbeitslos-arbeit-finden/arbeitslosengeld/gruendungszuschuss-beantragen).
- **Vermittlungsbudget:** may cover eligible application/interview-travel costs; it is discretionary, and BA advises applying before spending. Keep receipts. [BA guide](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/vermittlungsbudget).
- **Integration/language courses:** BAMF access, fees and exemptions depend on status/eligibility. [Integration courses](https://www.bamf.de/DE/Themen/Integration/ZugewanderteTeilnehmende/Integrationskurse/integrationskurse-node.html) and [cost FAQ](https://www.bamf.de/SharedDocs/FAQ/DE/Integrationskurse/006-was-kostet-ein-integrationskurs.html).

Prepare target role/city/contract type, language level and recognition steps, a realistic search schedule with application/interview examples, the specific barrier (course, translation or travel), and an estimate. Provide receipts only when requested. Ask about deadlines, proof and whether approval must precede spending; do not assume foreign work history counts or expenses will be reimbursed.

## Private search records and delayed decisions

Keep a factual private log of employers/roles contacted, dates/channels, CV/letter versions, interviews/travel costs, responses/follow-ups, and course discussions/approvals. Do not fabricate activity; ask the caseworker which efforts the plan requires. A local tracker is a reminder, not an official form.

If a claim or payment is delayed: check official messages; respond through eService/the named office before the deadline; explain any delay in writing; keep submitted forms/receipts; read appeal instructions and seek qualified advice quickly. Do not send benefit records to recruiters or agents.
