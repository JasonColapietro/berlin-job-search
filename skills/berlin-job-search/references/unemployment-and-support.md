# Unemployment and support

> Benefits depend on insurance history, residence, household, income, and the facts of the case. Contact the Agentur für Arbeit or Jobcenter directly; this overview is not a benefit decision.

## Arbeitsuchend and Arbeitslos registration

Register as **arbeitsuchend** no later than three months before a known end date, or within three days of learning that a job will end sooner. Register separately as **arbeitslos** by the first day without work. The two registrations are not interchangeable. See [BA: arbeitsuchend registration](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/arbeitslos-arbeit-finden/arbeitslosengeld/ihre-schritte-wenn-sie-arbeitslos-werden/wie-sie-sich-arbeitsuchend-melden) and [BA: arbeitslos registration](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/arbeitslos-arbeit-finden/arbeitslosengeld/ihre-schritte-wenn-sie-arbeitslos-werden/wie-sie-sich-arbeitslos-melden).

## Arbeitslosengeld I (ALG I)

ALG I is an insurance benefit. Typical eligibility includes being unemployed, available for insured employment of at least 15 hours per week, actively seeking work, registered unemployed, and meeting the qualifying insurance period. The standard qualifying period is generally 12 months of insured time in the 30 months before unemployment; short fixed-term exceptions and other credited periods can apply. The BA states a simplified replacement rate of **60%** of calculated net benefit earnings, or **67%** if the claimant or qualifying spouse/partner has a child. The amount is not simply a percentage of the last payslip. Entitlement duration depends on age and insured periods. Sources: [BA: entitlement, amount and duration](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/arbeitslos-arbeit-finden/arbeitslosengeld/finanzielle-hilfen/arbeitslosengeld-anspruch-hoehe-dauer) and [BA 2026 information leaflet](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/datei/merkblatt-fuer-arbeitslose_ba036520.pdf).

### ALG I preparation checklist

1. Complete the separate **Arbeitslosmeldung** and keep confirmation of its effective date.
2. Submit the ALG I claim through BA eServices or the responsible Agentur für Arbeit.
3. Collect employment dates, employer details, wage records, and any BA-requested documents; submit them only through the official service.
4. Tell the BA about relevant work and insurance periods, including periods that may have occurred outside Germany; the authority decides whether they count.
5. Keep evidence of job-search activity and attend required appointments. Ask the caseworker what proof and availability rules apply.

The amount is calculated from insured earnings and other statutory factors, not simply 60% or 67% of the last net payslip. The BA's current claim guidance is the reliable source for an individual estimate. [BA ALG I overview](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/arbeitslos-arbeit-finden/arbeitslosengeld/finanzielle-hilfen/arbeitslosengeld-anspruch-hoehe-dauer).

### During a claim

- Tell the Agentur für Arbeit promptly about changes to address, employment, health-related availability, or other facts it asks you to report.
- Keep copies of letters and note response deadlines. If a letter is unclear, ask the BA before the deadline rather than guessing.
- If a new job starts, tell the agency when the work begins; do not assume the employer's payroll report replaces your notification.
- If a benefit decision appears incorrect, read the appeal instructions (**Rechtsbehelfsbelehrung**) and seek advice promptly. Formal deadlines can be short. **(verify)** [BA eServices](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/eservices).

## Quitting, Aufhebungsvertrag, and Sperrzeit

Voluntarily ending employment, signing an Aufhebungsvertrag, or other conduct can cause a **Sperrzeit**. The BA says payment can be suspended for up to 12 weeks; a documented important reason may avoid it. Get individual advice before resigning or signing a termination agreement, and register on time. Source: [BA: ALG I and Sperrzeit](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/arbeitslos-arbeit-finden/arbeitslosengeld/finanzielle-hilfen/arbeitslosengeld-anspruch-hoehe-dauer).

Before making a decision:

- Compare a voluntary resignation, employer dismissal, and mutual termination agreement with a qualified adviser; each has different legal and financial consequences.
- Ask about notice period, remaining leave, release from duties, severance, and any non-compete before signing.
- If there is an important reason to leave, discuss what evidence the BA will need before ending the contract. Do not assume a reason is automatically sufficient.
- If the employer has issued a dismissal and you want to challenge it, the BA notes that a dismissal-protection claim normally has a three-week filing deadline. Get legal advice promptly. [BA: dismissal and severance](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/arbeitslos-arbeit-finden/arbeitslosengeld/das-muessen-sie-beachten/kuendigung-abfindung-freistellung).

## Grundsicherungsgeld / Jobcenter

From **1 July 2026**, the BA says Grundsicherungsgeld replaced Bürgergeld; older documents and common usage may still say Bürgergeld. Jobcenters assess need based on household income, assets, residence eligibility, and local housing costs. There is no single amount that applies to everyone. The BA's official [reform overview](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/grundsicherung-loest-buergergeld-ab) and [needs and components guide](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/grundsicherung/finanziell-absichern/bedarfe) describe the 2026 status. Eligibility for non-German nationals is case-specific; check with the Jobcenter.

### When to contact the Jobcenter

- If you do not qualify for ALG I, or ALG I is ending and you may need means-tested support, contact the Jobcenter promptly.
- Eligibility depends on the household (**Bedarfsgemeinschaft**), income/assets, residence conditions, and costs the local office considers appropriate; another person's award does not determine yours.
- Use the official application process for household and housing details. Share bank and identity information only through verified Jobcenter channels.
- Ask which current rules and reporting duties apply. Older letters may still say Bürgergeld during the 2026 transition. [BA: reform and current rules](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/grundsicherung-loest-buergergeld-ab).

## Training, job-search, and self-employment support

- **Bildungsgutschein:** may fund approved vocational training when necessary to end or prevent unemployment. Approval is individual; discuss it with the BA/Jobcenter before enrolling. [BA guide](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/karriere-und-weiterbildung/bildungsgutschein).
- **Gründungszuschuss:** for eligible ALG I recipients starting full-time self-employment. The BA describes a first phase of six months at the ALG I amount plus €300/month, followed by a possible nine months at €300/month. It is discretionary, needs a viable plan and remaining entitlement (usually at least 150 days at start), and should be discussed before starting. [BA guide](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/arbeitslos-arbeit-finden/arbeitslosengeld/gruendungszuschuss-beantragen).
- **Vermittlungsbudget:** may cover eligible application documents, interview travel, or other necessary job-search costs. It is discretionary; the BA advises applying before expenses arise. [BA guide](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/vermittlungsbudget).
- **Integration / language courses:** BAMF courses may be available depending on residence status and eligibility; fees and exemptions vary. Check [BAMF integration courses](https://www.bamf.de/DE/Themen/Integration/ZugewanderteTeilnehmende/Integrationskurse/integrationskurse-node.html) and the [cost FAQ](https://www.bamf.de/SharedDocs/FAQ/DE/Integrationskurse/006-was-kostet-ein-integrationskurs.html).

Ask the caseworker about eligibility, deadlines, required proof, and whether approval must precede spending. Do not assume international work history automatically counts toward a German benefit claim.

### A practical support request

When speaking with a caseworker, prepare a short, accurate plan:

1. Target role(s), city, and contract types.
2. Your current language levels and any qualification-recognition steps.
3. A realistic job-search schedule and examples of applications/interviews.
4. The specific barrier you need help with, such as an approved course, translation, or interview travel.
5. A cost estimate and receipts only when requested; ask whether approval must happen before the expense.

Do not buy a course or travel ticket expecting reimbursement without documented approval. The responsible authority makes the eligibility decision, not this guide.

## Keep job-search records

For ALG I or Jobcenter support, use a private, factual record of:

- Employers and role titles contacted.
- Application date, channel, and the version of the CV/letter sent.
- Interview dates, travel costs, and employer responses.
- Follow-up dates or a stated employer decision date.
- Training or language-course discussions and any written approval.

Do not fabricate activity to satisfy a requirement. Ask the caseworker which job-search efforts are expected for your plan and how to document them. Use the local tracker as a private reminder, not as a substitute for official forms.

## If an application or payment is delayed

1. Check the official account for a request for missing documents or an appointment notice.
2. Reply through the official eService or the listed office contact before the stated deadline.
3. If you cannot comply, contact the caseworker promptly and explain the reason in writing.
4. Keep copies of submitted forms and delivery confirmations.
5. If the decision is negative or incomplete, read its appeal instructions and seek qualified advice quickly.

The BA's [eServices portal](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/eservices) is the correct place to handle account tasks; do not send benefit records to a recruiter or to an agent's remote service.
