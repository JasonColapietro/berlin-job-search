# German job-ad and contract glossary

> Portions adapted from career-ops (MIT) — see NOTICE.md.

The explanations are practical reading aids, not legal interpretations. Check the complete clause and ask a qualified adviser when a term changes pay, immigration, or termination rights.

| Term | Meaning | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| (m/w/d) | männlich / weiblich / divers (male / female / diverse) | Standard inclusive job-ad marker; it does not signal a role-specific qualification. |
| (w/m/x), (f/m/x) | Alternative inclusive gender markers | Same practical meaning as m/w/d. |
| alle Geschlechter / all genders | All genders | Inclusive wording. |
| Festanstellung | Permanent employment relationship | Ask whether it is fixed-term or unbefristet. |
| unbefristet | Open-ended / not fixed-term | No scheduled end date; normal termination rules still apply. |
| befristet | Fixed-term | Check end date, reason or statutory basis, renewal, and notice rights. |
| Vollzeit | Full-time | Ask weekly hours and how overtime is handled. |
| Teilzeit | Part-time | Confirm weekly hours, schedule, and benefit pro-rating. |
| Werkstudent / Werkstudentin | Working student | Student status and work-hour/residence rules may apply. |
| Pflichtpraktikum | Mandatory internship required by study/training | Minimum-wage treatment differs by statutory conditions and duration; verify the contract and [Mindestlohngesetz](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/milog/__22.html). |
| freiwilliges Praktikum | Voluntary internship | Minimum-wage rules generally apply subject to statutory exceptions, including certain short orientation or study-related internships; verify. |
| Ausbildung | Vocational apprenticeship | Dual workplace and vocational-school training; pay is usually an Ausbildungsvergütung. |
| Azubi / Auszubildende | Apprentice / trainee in vocational training | Check training occupation, duration, chamber, school, and remuneration. |
| Quereinsteiger | Career changer | Employer may accept nontraditional experience; check required training and support. |
| Berufserfahrene | Experienced professional | Usually indicates demonstrated relevant experience. |
| Berufseinsteiger | Career starter | Entry-level or recent graduate. |
| Junior | Junior-level | Scope varies; do not assume it means zero experience. |
| Senior | Senior-level | Usually autonomy and scope, not only years of service. |
| verhandlungssicher | Negotiation-proficient | Strong professional language ability, often high B2/C1 context; ask what work situations require it. |
| fließend / fliessend | Fluent | Strong practical communication; exact CEFR level is not standardized by the phrase. |
| sehr gute Deutschkenntnisse | Very good German | Often substantial working-language expectation; clarify tasks and CEFR level. |
| gute Deutschkenntnisse | Good German | Ambiguous; ask whether meetings, documentation, or customers require German. |
| Grundkenntnisse | Basic knowledge | Limited foundation, usually not independent professional use. |
| Muttersprache | Native language | A language description, not a professional qualification. |
| Führerschein Klasse B | Category B driving licence | Usually standard passenger-car licence; check whether driving is essential and whether your foreign licence is valid. |
| Zeitarbeit | Temporary agency work | The agency may be your legal employer and assign you to client sites. |
| Arbeitnehmerüberlassung | Temporary agency work / labor leasing | Ask for agency, assignment, duration, pay, and collective terms. |
| Personaldienstleister | Staffing or recruitment service | Clarify whether the firm is recruiter, staffing agency, or employer. |
| im Auftrag unseres Kunden | On behalf of our client | Often an agency ad where the end-client is initially unnamed. |
| Initiativbewerbung | Unsolicited application | Contact an employer without a specific vacancy; target a team and contribution. |
| Eintrittstermin | Start date | State earliest realistic availability. |
| Gehaltsvorstellung | Salary expectation | Usually annual gross salary; state range if requested and define bonus assumptions. |
| Brutto | Gross pay | Before wage tax and employee contributions. |
| Netto | Net pay | Take-home amount after deductions; depends on personal circumstances. |
| Kündigungsfrist | Notice period | The time between termination notice and end date; check contract and statutory rules. |
| Probezeit | Probationary period | Check duration and notice period; statutory two-week notice can apply for an agreed period up to six months. |
| Freistellung | Release from work duties | Check whether paid, revocable, and how vacation/time credits are handled. |
| Aufhebungsvertrag | Mutual termination agreement | May affect ALG I; get advice before signing. |
| Abfindung | Severance payment | Not automatic; tax and benefit implications can apply. |
| mobiles Arbeiten | Mobile work | Work away from a fixed office; clarify place, equipment, reimbursement, and cross-border limits. |
| Homeoffice | Working from home | Check whether days are guaranteed or subject to approval. |
| hybrid | Hybrid work | Ask expected office days and whether policy can change. |
| Gleitzeit | Flexible working hours | Ask core hours, time recording, and balancing period. |
| Vertrauensarbeitszeit | Trust-based working time | Does not necessarily mean no working-time protections; ask how hours and overtime are recorded. |
| Tarifvertrag | Collective agreement | May set pay, hours, leave, notice, and other terms; ask which agreement and pay group apply. |
| TV-L | Collective agreement for many state public employees | Pay group and step depend on role and experience recognition. |
| TVöD | Collective agreement for many federal/municipal public employees | Check employer, division, pay group, and step. |
| IG Metall | Metalworkers' union / collective bargaining organization | The employer's actual agreement determines terms; not every company is covered. |
| AT-Gehalt | Above-tariff salary | Terms may be outside standard tariff tables; compare hours, bonus, and benefits. |
| Eingruppierung | Pay-group classification | Ask how duties and experience map to the applicable group/step. |
| bAV | Betriebliche Altersvorsorge / occupational pension | Ask employer contribution, vesting, fees, and portability. |
| VWL | Vermögenswirksame Leistungen | Employer-supported savings payment; details depend on plan and eligibility. |
| Jobrad | Employer bicycle-leasing benefit | Check salary conversion, insurance, maintenance, and exit terms. |
| Deutschlandticket | Nationwide local/regional public-transport pass | Clarify employer subsidy and whether it is a taxable benefit. |
| Jobticket | Employer-supported transit ticket | Coverage and subsidy depend on employer and local transit provider. |
| Urlaubsgeld | Holiday pay | Separate payment is not automatic; check contract or tariff. |
| Weihnachtsgeld | Christmas bonus | Check whether contractual, discretionary, conditional, or prorated. |
| Jahresbonus | Annual bonus | Ask target, formula, payout timing, and leaver rules. |
| VSOP | Virtual stock option plan | Contractual cash-settled or participation scheme; read vesting, exit, and tax terms. |
| Betriebsrat | Works council | Employee representation with statutory participation rights. |
| Betriebsvereinbarung | Works agreement | Agreement between employer and works council affecting workplace terms. |
| Arbeitszeugnis | Employment reference/certificate | Includes job history and often performance/conduct wording. |
| Zwischenzeugnis | Interim employment reference | May document current role and performance before employment ends. |
| Anmeldung | Residence registration | Register your address with the local municipality after moving. |
| Steuer-ID | Tax identification number | Personal tax ID; avoid posting or sending it to unverified contacts. |
| Sozialversicherungsnummer | Social-insurance number | Used for social-insurance reporting and pension records. |
| Krankenversicherung | Health insurance | Statutory/private eligibility and coverage depend on circumstances. |
| Aufenthaltsgenehmigung / Aufenthaltstitel | Residence permit/title | Read precise work conditions on the document and Zusatzblatt. |
| Zusatzblatt | Supplementary permit sheet | May specify employer, occupation, or work restrictions. |
| Anerkennung | Formal recognition of a qualification | Can be needed for regulated professions or particular permit routes. |
| Zeugnisbewertung | ZAB statement of comparability | Assesses a foreign higher-education qualification for certain uses. |
| Arbeitsuchendmeldung | Job-seeker registration | Separate from registration as unemployed; deadline may be three months / three days. |
| Arbeitslosmeldung | Registration as unemployed | Required separately, generally by the first day of unemployment. |
| Arbeitslosengeld I | Unemployment insurance benefit | Requires eligibility and registration; not means-tested like basic support. |
| Grundsicherungsgeld | Jobcenter basic-support benefit from July 2026 | Need-based support; former “Bürgergeld” may still appear in older information. |
| Vermittlungsbudget | Job-search cost support | Discretionary; apply before costs arise when the BA says so. |
| Bildungsgutschein | Training voucher | May fund approved training after individual assessment. |
| Beschäftigungsumfang | Employment fraction / scope | Check the percentage and weekly hours; a headline can hide part-time terms. |
| Wochenarbeitszeit | Weekly working hours | Compare actual paid hours, not just “full-time” or “part-time.” |
| Rufbereitschaft | On-call availability | Ask how often, response time, compensation, and whether time counts as working time. |
| Bereitschaftsdienst | Standby duty | Ask when you must be at a workplace and how standby hours are compensated. |
| Schichtarbeit | Shift work | Check rotation, night/weekend duties, and shift premiums. |
| Schichtzulage | Shift premium | A pay supplement for specified shifts; ask whether it is guaranteed and pensionable. |
| Dienstreise | Business trip | Clarify expected travel and which costs are reimbursed. |
| Reisebereitschaft | Willingness to travel | Ask for an approximate travel percentage and overnight frequency. |
| Standort | Work site / office location | Check the exact site and whether other locations may be assigned. |
| Einsatzort | Place of assignment | Often important in agency work, field service, or construction roles. |
| Außendienst | Field / external sales or service | Ask about territory, travel, car, and expense rules. |
| Innendienst | Office-based / internal sales or service | Usually office-centered; verify customer and phone workload. |
| Bewerbungsfrist | Application deadline | Submit early enough to resolve portal or document issues. |
| Auswahlverfahren | Selection process | Ask about interview rounds, tests, participants, and decision timing. |
| Bewerbungsgespräch | Job interview | Confirm format, language, time zone, and whether a task is expected. |
| Personalabteilung | Human-resources department | The HR contact may not be the hiring manager or the contract signatory. |
| Personalvermittlung | Recruitment / placement | Clarify whether the agency introduces you to a direct employer or employs you itself. |
| Arbeitsvertrag | Employment contract | Read the full written terms before signing; verbal promises may not appear in it. |
| Nachweis | Proof / documentary evidence | Ask which exact document is accepted and whether translation/certification is required. |
| Berufsqualifikation | Professional qualification | A foreign qualification may require assessment or formal recognition. |
| Hochschulabschluss | Higher-education degree | State the original degree and institution; equivalence is not automatic. |
| Zeugnis | Certificate / reference | Context matters: it may mean a school/degree certificate or an employment reference. |
| Gehaltsband | Salary band | Ask the level, range, and how experience determines placement. |
| Zulage | Allowance / supplement | Check whether it is fixed, conditional, recurring, or revocable. |
| Zuschlag | Premium / surcharge | Often tied to shifts, overtime, weekends, or specific working conditions. |
| Provision | Commission | Clarify target, formula, payout date, ramp-up, and clawback rules. |
| Leistungsprämie | Performance bonus | Ask for the formula and whether it is discretionary. |
| Befristungsgrund | Fixed-term reason | The legal basis may matter; review with qualified advice if unclear. |
| Datenschutz | Data protection / privacy | Read what CV and application data are stored, shared, and retained. |
| Präsenzpflicht | Mandatory on-site presence | Ask how often, at which site, and whether policy can change. |
| Pendelzeit | Commute time | Use actual door-to-door time at expected shift hours. |
| Umzugskosten | Relocation costs | Clarify eligible costs, reimbursement limit, and repayment conditions. |
| Tarifbindung | Collective-agreement coverage | Check whether the employer is bound and which agreement applies to the role. |
| Probeaufgabe | Interview exercise | Clarify time, scope, payment, and whether work will be used commercially. |
| Abwesenheitsnotiz | Out-of-office notice | May appear in correspondence; it does not indicate a rejection or job status. |
| Arbeitsort | Place of work | Compare the contract's stated site with “remote” or hybrid ad wording. |
| Arbeitsmittel | Work equipment | Ask which equipment is supplied and whether home-office costs are reimbursed. |
| Bereitschaft | Availability / standby | The exact meaning depends on whether it is on-call duty or general schedule flexibility. |
| Einarbeitung | Onboarding / initial training | Ask who supports you and how long the ramp-up period is expected to last. |
| Probezeitgespräch | Probation review | Ask when feedback is given and which goals are assessed. |
| Absage | Rejection | Keep the message and close or update the tracker entry; a polite request for feedback is optional. |
| Zusage | Acceptance / positive offer | Confirm the final written conditions and start date before resigning elsewhere. |
| Vertragsangebot | Written contract offer | It may still be conditional; read the full terms and any attachments. |
| Gehaltsabrechnung | Payslip | Check gross amounts, tax class, social-insurance deductions, and any benefits. |
| Lohnsteuer | Wage tax | Withholding through payroll; it is not the same as final annual tax liability. |
| Sozialversicherung | Social insurance | Contributions cover statutory systems; exact deductions depend on pay and status. |
| Arbeitgeberanteil | Employer contribution | Employer-paid social-insurance share; not additional net pay. |
| Bruttogehalt | Gross salary | Compare on an annual basis and normalize for weekly hours. |
| Jahresarbeitszeit | Annual working time | Check how holidays, overtime, and time-account balances are counted. |
| Gutschrift | Credit / accrued balance | Could refer to time account, expenses, or compensation; ask for the exact ledger. |
| Abrechnung | Settlement / payroll calculation | Review the document against the contract and ask HR/payroll about discrepancies. |

| Arbeitsbescheinigung | Employer statement of employment data | The BA may request it to assess ALG I; check reported dates and pay if something is wrong. |
| Verfügbarkeit | Availability for work | A condition in ALG I rules; tell the BA about relevant restrictions. |
| Eigenbemühungen | Your own job-search efforts | Keep a factual record of applications and contacts if the agency requests it. |
| Bedarfsgemeinschaft | Household assessed together for basic support | Household composition can affect Jobcenter need calculations. |
| Kosten der Unterkunft | Housing and heating costs in a benefit assessment | The Jobcenter applies local rules; ask about recognized costs for your area. |
| Arbeitszeitkonto | Working-time account | Check how overtime/undertime is recorded, balanced, and paid at exit. |
| Kurzarbeit | Short-time work | Reduced working time under a statutory scheme; ask how pay and availability are affected. |
| Kündigungsschutzklage | Dismissal-protection claim | A legal challenge to dismissal; short filing deadlines may apply. |
| Meldepflicht | Duty to report a change or attend an appointment | Confirm the required channel and deadline in the authority's notice. |
| Leistungsentgelt | Calculated net reference amount for ALG I | It is not the same as take-home pay; use the BA's benefit calculation. |
| Wiedereingliederung | Gradual return-to-work process | Clarify employer, health-insurer, and authority responsibilities before work resumes. |
| Wiedervorlage | Follow-up / reminder date | Use it for a recruiter response date or an official document deadline. |
| Nachreichfrist | Deadline to provide missing documents | Ask the authority whether a deadline can be extended before it passes. |
| Arbeitsunfähigkeitsbescheinigung | Certificate of incapacity for work | Follow the employer/insurer process if illness affects an interview or work start. |
| Personalfragebogen | Employer's personal-data questionnaire | Complete it only through the verified employer and provide only requested information. |
| Tätigkeitsbeschreibung | Job-duty description | Compare it with the title; actual duties may affect pay group, recognition, or permit review. |
| Eingangsbestätigung | Confirmation of receipt | Save it, but do not treat it as acceptance or approval of an application. |
| Absagegrund | Reason for rejection | You may ask for feedback politely; do not assume every employer must provide it. |

More sources: [BA occupation glossary](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/lexikon), [§622 BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__622.html), [minimum-wage internship rules, §22 MiLoG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/milog/__22.html), and [BA job-seeker registration](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/arbeitslos-arbeit-finden/arbeitslosengeld/ihre-schritte-wenn-sie-arbeitslos-werden/wie-sie-sich-arbeitsuchend-melden).
