## Gross, net and package

- **Brutto** is gross; **Netto** is after tax and employee social contributions. Estimate with the [Federal Ministry of Finance wage-tax calculator](https://www.bmf-steuerrechner.de/), not as a payroll promise. Tax classes I–VI affect withholding, not by themselves a couple's final annual tax.
- In 2026 the statutory minimum wage is **€13.90 gross/hour**; the regular Minijob limit is **€603/month** (€7,236 at the monthly limit for a full year). Sources: [MiLoV5](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/milov5/__1.html), [BA 2026 notice](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/datei/weisung-202604002_ba056519.pdf), [Minijob-Zentrale](https://www.minijob-zentrale.de/DE/die-minijobs).
- **13th salary/Weihnachtsgeld** is not automatic; check whether it is guaranteed, discretionary or conditional. For bonus/commission, ask target, formula, history, pro-rating, eligibility and clawback.
- **VSOP/equity**: review vesting, strike price, dilution, exercise window, leaver terms and tax; paper value is not guaranteed cash.
- **bAV/VWL**: compare employer contribution, vesting, fees and portability. Also check Jobticket/Jobrad, leave, training and flexibility.
- The analyzer annualizes monthly amounts with 12 months (never 13); hourly comparisons use approximately 2,080 hours/year (40 × 52), not an assertion about a particular schedule. Immigration salary thresholds may differ from market pay; see [visa and work permits](visa-and-work-permits.md).

| Offer wording | Normalize / clarify |
|---|---|
| €4,500 gross/month | €54,000 gross/year at 12 payments; confirm extra payments. |
| €70,000 base + target bonus | €70,000 guaranteed plus contingent bonus. |
| €80,000 for 32 hours/week | Confirm whether already prorated; compare actual hours. |
| €18/hour | Annual equivalent depends on hours/weeks; 2,080 is only a 40-hour reference. |
| Salary range + VSOP | Compare cash separately; value equity only after reviewing grant and exit terms. |

## State or ask for a range

If asked, state annual gross euros for the target hours, your genuine range and minimum, and whether variable pay/equity is included. Do not disclose former salary unless legally required. If an ad asks for a number without a range, give a reasoned range rather than a single regretful minimum; clarify annual/monthly gross and hours (40-hour and 32-hour offers are not directly comparable).

**English:** “For a full-time role with this scope, I am targeting €82,000–€90,000 gross annually, depending on the responsibilities and total package.”

**Deutsch:** “Für eine Vollzeitstelle mit diesem Verantwortungsbereich liegt meine Gehaltsvorstellung bei 82.000 bis 90.000 Euro brutto jährlich, abhängig von Aufgaben und Gesamtpaket.”

If the ad gives no salary, ask for the budget before several interview rounds:

**English:** “Could you share the budgeted annual gross range for this position? That will help me confirm we are aligned before the next interview.”

**Deutsch:** “Können Sie mir bitte die vorgesehene Jahresbruttospanne für diese Position nennen? So kann ich vor der nächsten Gesprächsrunde prüfen, ob unsere Vorstellungen zusammenpassen.”

## Negotiate and compare

Base a request on role scope, location, verified experience and evidence. Prepare a target range, minimum guaranteed base, two or three real achievements, package questions (bonus, equity, bAV, VWL, leave, commute, remote terms) and flexible vs non-negotiable items.

> Thank you for the offer. I am excited about the role. Based on the responsibilities and my experience in [specific relevant area], I was targeting €[range] gross annually. Is there flexibility to bring the base salary closer to €[target]? I would also like to understand the bonus, pension contribution, and equity details.

> Vielen Dank für das Angebot. Ich freue mich sehr über die Möglichkeit. Auf Grundlage des Verantwortungsbereichs und meiner Erfahrung in [konkreter Bereich] hatte ich an ein Jahresbruttogehalt von [Spanne] Euro gedacht. Gibt es Spielraum, das Grundgehalt näher an [Zielbetrag] Euro anzupassen? Außerdem würde ich gern die Details zu Bonus, betrieblicher Altersvorsorge und Beteiligung verstehen.

If base pay is fixed, discuss a guaranteed sign-on bonus, review date, leave, training budget or remote terms. Keep final terms in writing. Do not volunteer salary history; redirect to role scope and expectations. A visa salary check uses qualifying pay, not speculative bonus/equity; confirm components with the competent authority.
