Only a minority of ads state a salary. A Job Drop Berlin snapshot on 23 October 2026 reported 17% of live Berlin startup roles with salary information (395/2,361); its denominator includes only listings carrying the relevant field. [Daily Drop methodology and figures](https://jobdropberlin.com/daily). A general 8–17% range varies by board and sample; treat it as `(verify)`, not a national statistic.

Use at least three comparison points: occupation/region, seniority and sector, and employer-specific compensation. Official occupational data is available in the [BA Entgeltatlas](https://web.arbeitsagentur.de/entgeltatlas/). Other useful cross-checks include [kununu Gehaltscheck](https://www.kununu.com/de/gehalt), [gehalt.de](https://www.gehalt.de/), and the current [StepStone Gehaltsreport](https://www.stepstone.de/e-recruiting/hr-wissen/gehalt/gehaltsreport-deutschlands-gehaelter-im-fokus/).

### How to compare benchmarks

- Compare the same role family, seniority, city/region, and employment basis. Berlin startup self-reports, company-level compensation, and national occupational medians are different populations.
- Separate base salary from total compensation (TC). Levels.fyi often includes equity/bonus; a cash-only offer should not be compared to TC without adjustment.
- Check sample size, reporting period, job-level definitions, and whether figures are self-reported or modeled.
- Use a range rather than a single percentile as a personal target; responsibility, language scope, and company stage can shift the right band.
- Treat online salary estimates as negotiation inputs, not guarantees of an employer's budget or legal advice.

## Gross-to-net and pay components

- **Brutto** is gross pay; **Netto** is after taxes and employee social-insurance contributions. Use a current [Federal Ministry of Finance wage-tax calculator](https://www.bmf-steuerrechner.de/) or an independent gross-net calculator as an estimate, not a payroll promise.
- Germany has tax classes I–VI for wage-tax withholding. Tax class affects monthly withholding; it does not alone determine a couple's final annual income-tax liability. Check with the tax authority or a tax professional.
- In 2026, the statutory minimum wage is **€13.90 gross/hour**; the regular Minijob earnings limit is **€603/month** (€7,236 over a full year at the monthly limit). See the [MiLoV5 statutory text](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/milov5/__1.html), [BA 2026 notice](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/datei/weisung-202604002_ba056519.pdf), and [Minijob-Zentrale](https://www.minijob-zentrale.de/DE/die-minijobs).
- **13th salary / Weihnachtsgeld** is not automatic; ask whether it is guaranteed, discretionary, or conditional and whether it is included in annual gross.
- **Bonus**: clarify target amount, payout history, eligibility, pro-rating, clawback rules, and what happens if you leave before payment.
- **VSOP/equity**: ask for instrument type, vesting, strike price, dilution, exercise window, leaver terms, and tax treatment. Do not treat paper value as guaranteed cash.
- **bAV** (occupational pension) and **VWL** (capital-forming benefits) can add value, but compare employer contributions, vesting, fees, and portability.
- The analyzer converts monthly amounts to annual values using 12 months (never 13). Hourly salary comparisons use an approximate 2,080 hours/year (40 hours × 52 weeks); this is only an estimate and does not assert a full-time schedule.
- A salary threshold for an immigration route is not necessarily the same as market-rate compensation; see [visa and work permits](visa-and-work-permits.md).

### Normalize offers before comparing

| Offer wording | Normalize it as |
|---|---|
| €4,500 gross/month | €54,000 gross/year (12 months); confirm whether any extra payment is guaranteed. |
| €70,000 base + target bonus | €70,000 guaranteed base plus a separate contingent bonus; do not treat the target as guaranteed. |
| €80,000 for a 32-hour week | Compare against the actual weekly hours and confirm whether the amount is already prorated. |
| €18/hour | An approximate annual equivalent depends on contracted hours and paid weeks; the analyzer uses 2,080 hours only as a rough 40-hour reference. |
| Salary range plus VSOP | Compare cash salary separately; value VSOP only after reviewing the grant and exit terms. |

When an employer quotes a monthly amount, ask whether it is 12 equal payments, includes holiday or Christmas money, or is subject to variable pay. The words **brutto**, **Grundgehalt**, and **Jahresgehalt** should be explicit in a written offer.

## How to state a Gehaltsvorstellung

If asked, state **annual gross salary in euros** for the target working hours. Offer a range only when it is genuinely negotiable and know your minimum. Clarify whether the range includes guaranteed variable pay, sign-on bonus, or equity.

**English:** “For a full-time role with this scope, I am targeting €82,000–€90,000 gross annually, depending on the responsibilities and total package.”

**Deutsch:** “Für eine Vollzeitstelle mit diesem Verantwortungsbereich liegt meine Gehaltsvorstellung bei 82.000 bis 90.000 Euro brutto jährlich, abhängig von Aufgaben und Gesamtpaket.”

Do not disclose a former salary unless legally required (and salary-history practice is changing). The [EU Pay Transparency Directive](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2023/970/oj/eng) sets a 7 June 2026 transposition deadline. As of 23 October 2026, the Linklaters country tracker still lists Germany under official announcement rather than completed transposition, and a June 2026 EU Commission report said the deadline had passed. Germany's implementing status and practical rights are dynamic; **(verify)** current national law before relying on any new interview right. Sources: [Linklaters tracker](https://www.linklaters.com/insights/thought-leadership/eu-pay-transparency-directive/the-eu-pay-transparency-directive-implementation-tracker), [European Commission Germany statement](https://germany.representation.ec.europa.eu/nachrichten-und-veranstaltungen/pressemitteilungen/eu-kommission-fordert-eu-weite-umsetzung-der-vorschriften-zur-lohngleichheit-2026-06-08_de).

## When a job ad gives no salary

If compensation is omitted, estimate a range from the role level, market, hours, and verified experience. Ask the recruiter for the employer's budget before investing in several interview rounds.

**English:** “Could you share the budgeted annual gross range for this position? That will help me confirm we are aligned before the next interview.”

**Deutsch:** “Können Sie mir bitte die vorgesehene Jahresbruttospanne für diese Position nennen? So kann ich vor der nächsten Gesprächsrunde prüfen, ob unsere Vorstellungen zusammenpassen.”

When an ad asks for a number but gives no range, state a reasoned range, not a single minimum you would regret. A 40-hour full-time target is not automatically comparable to a 32-hour offer; calculate the actual hours and clarify whether a quoted amount is annual gross or monthly gross.

## Compare the guaranteed package first

| Component | Guaranteed? | How to compare |
|---|---|---|
| Base salary | Usually contractual | Annual gross, actual weekly hours, and pay group. |
| Bonus / commission | Often conditional | Target, formula, history, pro-rating, and payout conditions. |
| Equity / VSOP | Often contingent | Vesting, dilution, leaver terms, and cash-out conditions. |
| Pension / bAV | Depends on plan | Employer contribution, vesting, fees, and portability. |
| Mobility / Jobticket | Depends on employer | Monthly value, tax treatment, and whether it replaces another benefit. |
| Leave and flexibility | Contract/policy | Days, office expectation, and any change rights. |

Do not add a target bonus or speculative equity to base salary when checking a visa salary threshold. Confirm qualifying pay components with the competent authority.

## Negotiation scripts

Base your number on scope, experience, location, and evidence. Keep a collaborative tone and ask for the full package.

**English**

> Thank you for sharing the offer. I am excited about the role. Based on the responsibilities and my experience in [specific relevant area], I was targeting €[range] gross annually. Is there flexibility to bring the base salary closer to €[target]? I would also like to understand the bonus, pension contribution, and equity details.

**Deutsch**

> Vielen Dank für das Angebot. Ich freue mich sehr über die Möglichkeit. Auf Grundlage des Verantwortungsbereichs und meiner Erfahrung in [konkreter Bereich] hatte ich an ein Jahresbruttogehalt von [Spanne] Euro gedacht. Gibt es Spielraum, das Grundgehalt näher an [Zielbetrag] Euro anzupassen? Außerdem würde ich gern die Details zu Bonus, betrieblicher Altersvorsorge und Beteiligung verstehen.

If the base is fixed, negotiate another concrete element: guaranteed signing bonus, review date, vacation, training budget, or remote arrangement. Ask for final terms in writing and take time to review them.

### Negotiation preparation

Before a compensation call, write down:

1. **Target:** a reasonable annual gross range for the role.
2. **Minimum:** the lowest guaranteed base that still works for the candidate.
3. **Evidence:** two or three real achievements tied to the scope.
4. **Package items:** bonus, equity, bAV, VWL, leave, commute, and remote terms to clarify.
5. **Trade-offs:** which item is flexible and which is a deal-breaker.

Do not volunteer a current salary merely because an interviewer asks. If you choose not to disclose it, redirect to the scope and expected range. Any pay-history rights depend on current law; see the `(verify)` note on German implementation of the [EU Pay Transparency Directive](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2023/970/oj/eng).
